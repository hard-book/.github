# Hardbook

**The booking page that does the rest.**

Hardbook is online booking software that works as one link. Your client opens it, picks the dates and books. No app, no account, no password.

You decide what it takes to book you. A signed contract, a deposit, a few questions, a package: each one is a switch. Some jobs need all of them, some need none. The page carries your name, colours, images and voice, so your client books you, not a tool.

Deposits go straight to your own Stripe account. Hardbook adds no fee. The free plan runs the whole booking: no limit on bookings, plus contracts, deposits, packages, questions, reminders and Google Calendar sync. Pro is $19 a month or $190 a year. It removes the "Powered by Hardbook" line and adds motion identity, full analytics and 30 AI contract drafts a month.

Wedding photographers and videographers, film and video crew, editors and DJs use it to book whole days. Hardbook is not a CRM or an invoicing suite. It gets you booked.
