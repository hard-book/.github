# Hardbook

**Booking and contract signing for day-rate freelancers.**

Hardbook turns a verbal "sounds good" into a locked, signed booking. You send one link. Your client picks the dates, signs your contract and, if you want, pays a deposit, all on one page. Nothing is confirmed until the contract is signed. Your client needs no app and no account. Hardbook is not a CRM, a project manager or an invoicing suite. It does one job. The free plan has no limit on bookings. Built by a freelancer.
